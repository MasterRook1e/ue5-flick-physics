# Governance

Flick Physics is currently maintained by one primary maintainer. Repository decisions are
made in public issues and pull requests whenever they affect users or public contracts.

## Principles

1. Prefer verified usefulness over feature count.
2. Preserve a small game-agnostic boundary.
3. Keep one tested numerical source of truth.
4. Treat compatibility and performance as evidence-based claims.
5. Prefer backward-compatible additions before 1.0, while documenting unavoidable breaks.
6. Welcome external review without manufacturing activity or adoption signals.

## Becoming a maintainer

A recurring contributor may be invited after sustained, high-quality participation in
review, issue triage, compatibility verification, documentation, and implementation.
Repository access is based on demonstrated stewardship rather than commit volume.

## Changes to governance

Material changes use a pull request and should remain open long enough for active
contributors to comment. The primary maintainer resolves the decision while the project
has a single-maintainer structure.
